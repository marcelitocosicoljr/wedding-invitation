# Audio Assets

Place your wedding background music file here:

## Required file:
- **`wedding-music.mp3`** — The romantic instrumental that auto-plays as background music

## Recommendations:
- Format: MP3 (best compatibility) or OGG
- Duration: 3–5 minutes (it loops automatically)
- Style: Soft romantic instrumental (piano, strings, acoustic guitar)

## Free royalty-free sources:
- **Pixabay Music** — https://pixabay.com/music/search/romantic/
- **Free Music Archive** — https://freemusicarchive.org/
- **Bensound** — https://www.bensound.com/royalty-free-music/romantic

## Usage:
Simply download your chosen track, rename it to `wedding-music.mp3`, and place it in this `/public/audio/` directory.
The music player will automatically pick it up.
